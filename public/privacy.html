Privacy

Short version: Ratio runs in your browser. The one exception is Share Link.

What stays on your device

Your colors, target, batch size, and everything else you enter is only ever kept in your browser's localStorage, so it comes back the next time you open the app on that device. It's never sent anywhere unless you click Share.

What Share Link stores

Clicking Share sends that one palette to our server, which stores:

  • the target color and each paint's color and name (whatever you've typed into the name fields)
  • the batch size and unit you had selected

That's it — no account, no email, no browsing history, nothing tied to who you are. It's kept for 30 days from the last time anyone opens the link (each visit resets that clock), then it's automatically deleted. Anyone with the link can view and copy it into their own editable palette; nothing about your original session changes when they do.

IP addresses

When you create a share link, your IP address is briefly used to prevent abuse (limiting how many links one address can create per hour). It's held in memory for that check and is never written to storage or logged alongside your palette data.

No tracking

No analytics, no cookies, no third-party scripts, no ads.

Verify it yourself

Ratio is open source — don't just take this page's word for it. The frontend (src/lib/share-link.ts) and the Share Link backend (server/) are both in the repo, so you can read exactly what gets sent and stored: github.com/Official-Husko/ratio-color-mixer .
